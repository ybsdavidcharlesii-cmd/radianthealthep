Frequently Asked Questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. Call 609-766-4345 or use our contact page to get on the schedule.

What ages do you see?

We provide primary care and preventive medicine for patients ages 13 and up.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes, telehealth visits are available for many types of care.

Do you offer mobile care?

Mobile medical care is available for eligible homebound patients. See our Mobile Care page for details.

How do I schedule a visit?

Visits are by appointment. Call, email, or use the contact page and we will get you on the schedule.

Insurance

Which insurance plans do you accept?

Please call 609-766-4345 or email care@radianthealthnp.org and we will confirm whether your plan is accepted.

What if my plan isn't accepted?

Reach out to us and we will talk through your options.
