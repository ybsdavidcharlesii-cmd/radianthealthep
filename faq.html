FAQs & Insurance

Frequently Asked Questions

Personalized Care. Meaningful Relationships. Better Health. Explore our frequently asked questions to learn more about our services and what to expect.

Getting Started

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes! We are excited to welcome new patients to our practice. Our goal is to provide compassionate, comprehensive, and personalized primary care to help you achieve and maintain your best health.

How do I schedule my first appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling our office at 609-766-4345. Our team will be happy to assist you.

Appointments & Office Visits

Do you offer same-day or sick visits?

We understand that unexpected health concerns arise. We strive to accommodate urgent medical needs with same-day or next-available appointments whenever possible. Please call our office to discuss availability.

Do you offer telehealth appointments?

Yes, we offer convenient telehealth visits for certain medical concerns, follow up appointments, and medication management when appropriate.

Insurance & Payments

What insurance plans do you accept?

We accept Medicare, Medicaid, and UnitedHealthcare. Please contact our office to confirm participation with your specific insurance plan before scheduling.

What if I don’t have health insurance?

We welcome the opportunity to discuss available self-pay options. Please contact our office for information about visit fees and payment policies.

After-Hours & Emergencies

What should I do if I become sick after office hours?

Please contact 609-766-4345 for guidance about non-emergency concerns. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

The Radiant Difference

What makes Radiant Health and Wellness different?

At Radiant Health and Wellness, we believe exceptional healthcare begins with meaningful relationships. We understand that every patient has a unique story, individual healthcare needs, and personal wellness goals. That’s why we take the time to listen, understand your concerns, and work collaboratively with you to create personalized care plans.

Personalized Care

Meaningful Relationships

Better Health

We believe healthcare is not just about treating illness. It’s about building a lasting partnership that helps you live a healthier, more fulfilling life.

Ready to Begin Your Healthcare Journey?

Your health matters. Your relationship with your provider matters. We’re committed to making your healthcare experience personal, supportive, and convenient. We look forward to welcoming you!
