Protect Yourself and Your Loved Ones This Flu Season

Your health matters. Prevention starts with vaccination.

As flu season approaches, we want to remind our patients and their families of the importance of staying up to date with their Flu and COVID-19 vaccinations.

Vaccination is one of the most effective ways to reduce the risk of serious illness, hospitalization, and complications from these respiratory viruses. Staying protected is especially important for older adults, individuals with chronic health conditions, and those with weakened immune systems.

We encourage all eligible patients to speak with their healthcare provider about receiving their recommended Flu and COVID-19 vaccines.

Need a Vaccine at Home?

If you have difficulty leaving your home, please contact your local Health Department to ask about scheduling an in-home vaccination appointment. Home vaccination services may be available depending on your location and eligibility.

Stay healthy. Stay protected. Get vaccinated.
