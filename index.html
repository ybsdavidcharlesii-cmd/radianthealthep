Welcome to Radiant Health and Wellness

Healthcare should be personal.

At Radiant Health and Wellness, we believe the best care begins with a strong provider-patient relationship built on trust, communication, and continuity.

As a solo primary care practice, we provide individualized care for adolescents, adults, and seniors, focusing on prevention, wellness, and the management of acute and chronic health conditions.

Our goal is not only to treat illness but to help you achieve and maintain your highest level of health.

Our Services

Comprehensive Primary Care Services

From prevention to chronic disease management — in our office, through telehealth, or in your home.

Preventive Care & Wellness

Annual physicals, screenings, vaccinations, Medicare wellness visits, and lifestyle counseling.

Chronic Disease Management

Ongoing care for diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, thyroid disorders, asthma, and COPD.

Adolescent & Adult Care

Care for ages 13 and older — school and sports physicals, women's health, and men's health.

Acute Care

Sick visits, minor illnesses and injuries, infections, and urgent health concerns.

Telehealth Services

Virtual follow-ups, medication management, and convenient access to care from home.

Mobile Primary Care

Healthcare delivered where you are — comprehensive primary care in the home for eligible homebound patients.
