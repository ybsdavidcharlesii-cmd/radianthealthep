About Me

Care rooted in genuine connection

I started Radiant Health and Wellness to give patients in Ewing the kind of unhurried, personal care that's become hard to find — care that treats you as a whole person, not just a chart.

My Story

Why I opened Radiant Health and Wellness

After years in clinical practice, I saw the same pattern again and again: rushed visits, fragmented records, and patients who felt like a number. Radiant Health and Wellness is my answer to that — a practice built around real relationships, thoughtful care, and modern tools that actually make life easier for patients.

Every visit here starts with listening. Whether you're coming in for a routine checkup or navigating a more complex health concern, my goal is the same: to help you feel informed, respected, and genuinely cared for.

  • Whole-person, relationship-based approach to primary care
  • Focus on prevention, education, and long-term wellness
  • Secure EHR patient portal for records and messaging
  • Conveniently located at 25 Scotch Road, Suite 5, Ewing, NJ
