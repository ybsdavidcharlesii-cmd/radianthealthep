Mobile Primary Care

Bringing Healthcare to You

For patients who are homebound or have difficulty traveling to medical appointments, Radiant Health and Wellness offers mobile primary care services in the comfort of your home.

How It Works

Care that meets you where you are

We understand that transportation challenges, chronic illness, mobility limitations, and caregiving responsibilities can make accessing healthcare difficult.

Our mobile care program helps ensure patients continue to receive the medical attention they need while maintaining comfort and dignity.

Contact Us About Eligibility →

Mobile Services May Include:

  • Routine primary care visits
  • Chronic disease management
  • Medication reviews
  • Preventive care
  • Hospital follow-up visits
  • Care coordination
  • Wellness assessments
