Services

Care designed around your whole health

From everyday primary care to a modern EHR patient portal, Radiant Health and Wellness gives you the tools and support to stay well.

🩺

Primary Care

Annual physicals, preventive screenings, chronic condition management, and same-practice continuity you can count on.

🌿

Wellness Planning

Personalized guidance on nutrition, lifestyle, and preventive health — built around your goals, not a generic checklist.

💊

Chronic Care Management

Ongoing support for conditions like diabetes, hypertension, and more, with a care plan that evolves with you.

💻

EHR System Integration

Your records, visit notes, and test results live in a secure electronic health record system — accessible anytime through your patient portal.

💬

Secure Messaging

Message your care team directly through the patient portal for quick, non-urgent questions and follow-ups.

📅

Flexible Scheduling

Appointments designed to fit real schedules, including afternoon and early-evening availability.

EHR Patient Portal

Modern technology, personal care.

Radiant Health and Wellness runs on an integrated electronic health record (EHR) system, so your information is organized, secure, and always accessible to you.

  • View lab results and visit summaries online
  • Message your care team directly and securely
  • Request appointments and prescription refills
  • Keep your full health history in one place
